# Quick Build Cheatsheet - WizBiz

## 🎯 Top 3 Recommended Builds

### 1. CRYSTAL RUSH ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (EASIEST - RECOMMENDED)
```
L1: Lightning    L2: Earth       L3: Air
L4: Lightning II L5: Earth II    L6: FUSION (Earth + Lightning = CRYSTAL)
L7: Arcane       L8: Crystal II  L9: Air II      L10: Arcane II

SLOTS: Crystal | Lightning | Air | Arcane
```
**Why**: Only 1 fusion, all primaries, 8-needle pierce = crowd destroyer
**Best for**: All players, farming, crowd control

---

### 2. METEOR HUNTER ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (BOSS KILLER)
```
L1: Earth        L2: Arcane      L3: Fire
L4: Lightning    L5: FUSION (Earth + Arcane = GRAVITY)
L6: FUSION (Gravity + Fire = METEOR)
L7: Meteor II    L8: Air         L9: Meteor III  L10: Air II

SLOTS: Meteor | Lightning | Air | Fire
```
**Why**: 4.0 damage homing + AOE, Tier 3 = 8.0 effective damage!
**Best for**: Boss rushing, elite hunting, late game

---

### 3. LIGHTNING BLITZ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (NO FUSIONS - SIMPLE)
```
L1: Lightning    L2: Air         L3: Arcane
L4: Lightning II L5: Air II      L6: Arcane II
L7: Lightning III L8: Air III    L9: Arcane III  L10: Fire

SLOTS: Lightning III | Air III | Arcane III | Fire
```
**Why**: No fusions needed, tier to 3 fast, reliable
**Best for**: Beginners, speedruns, consistent damage

## 📊 Element Tier List

### S-TIER (Always Pick)
- ⚡ **Lightning**: 2.0 dmg, 1500ms, chains (PRIMARY)
- 💨 **Air**: 1.5 dmg, 1200ms FASTEST, AOE (PRIMARY)
- ✨ **Arcane**: 3.0 dmg, homing + boomerang (PRIMARY)
- 💎 **Crystal**: 2.5 dmg, 2000ms, 8-needle pierce (FUSION: Earth + Lightning)
- 🔥 **Meteor**: 4.0 dmg HIGHEST, homing, AOE (FUSION: Gravity + Fire)

### A-TIER (Very Good)
- 🌍 **Earth**: 3.0 dmg, knockback (PRIMARY)
- 🔥 **Fire**: 2.0 dmg, burn DoT, group bonus (PRIMARY)
- ⚡ **Thunder**: 3.5 dmg, 3 bounces (FUSION)
- 💥 **Blast**: 3.5 dmg, 250px AOE (FUSION: Fire + Air)
- ⚡ **Chaos**: Variable, fast 1400ms (FUSION: Fire + Lightning)

### B-TIER (Situational)
- 💧 **Water**: 1.5 dmg, slow effect (PRIMARY - for fusions)
- ❄️ **Ice**: 2.5 dmg, freeze (FUSION)
- ✨ **Holy**: 3.0 dmg, undead bonus (FUSION)
- 🌊 **Storm**: Variable, tornado (FUSION: Water + Air)
- 🪨 **Rock**: 2.0 dmg, 30% crit (FUSION)

### C-TIER (Avoid)
- ☠️ **Poison**: 1.0 dmg LOWEST (needs DoT fix)
- 🌑 **Void**: 500ms cooldown TOO SLOW
- 🔥 **Lava**: 600ms cooldown terrible
- 🌍 **Mud**: 3000ms too slow, use Earth instead

## 💡 Passive Priority

**Always Pick (In Order)**:
1. 🔄 **Phoenix Heart** - First revive (ESSENTIAL)
2. ➕ **Extra Slot** - 6th element slot
3. 📏 **Magnitude** - AOE +20% size
4. ⚔️ **Power Amplification** - Damage +10%
5. 🏃 **Swift Stride** - Move speed +15%
6. 📦 **Passive Slot Increase** - More passives

**Skip**:
- ❤️ **Vitality Surge** (only if dying often)
- Other passives (situational)

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **DON'T pick Water early** (only 1.5 damage, use for fusions only)
❌ **DON'T rush fusions** (tier primaries to 3 first!)
❌ **DON'T skip Phoenix Heart** (revive is #1 passive)
❌ **DON'T fuse away Lightning** (needed for Crystal)
❌ **DON'T pick Poison** (1.0 damage is terrible)
❌ **DON'T ignore tiers** (Tier 5 = 3x damage!)

✅ **DO tier your main DPS** to 5 ASAP
✅ **DO keep Lightning** (best primary for chains)
✅ **DO get Crystal** (easiest S-tier fusion)
✅ **DO prioritize Air** (fastest fire rate)
✅ **DO take Phoenix Heart** at first opportunity
✅ **DO learn 2-3 fusion recipes** (Crystal, Meteor, Chaos)
