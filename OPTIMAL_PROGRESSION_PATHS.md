# Optimal Progression Paths for WizBiz

## Executive Summary

This guide analyzes **effectiveness vs effort** for all element paths and provides optimal build strategies for different playstyles.

### Key Metrics
- **Effectiveness**: DPS, utility, survivability, boss damage
- **Effort**: Number of fusions required, element dependencies
- **Tier Impact**: Tier 5 elements deal 3x damage (critical for endgame)

## Part 1: Early Game (Levels 1-5)

### Goal: Build DPS Foundation

#### **TIER S: Best Starting Elements**

**1. Lightning (Primary - No Fusion Required)**
- **Effectiveness**: 9/10
- **Effort**: 1/10 (direct level-up reward)
- **Why**: 2.0 damage, 1500ms cooldown (fast), chains to 2 enemies
- **Strategy**: Pick at level 1-2, focus on tiering up to Tier 3+
- **Synergy**: Great for crowd control, combos well with everything

**2. Air (Primary - No Fusion Required)**
- **Effectiveness**: 8/10
- **Effort**: 1/10 (direct level-up reward)
- **Why**: 1200ms cooldown (FASTEST), AOE knockback, 150px radius
- **Strategy**: Best DPS primary element, spam-friendly
- **Note**: Slightly low damage (1.5) compensated by fire rate

**3. Arcane (Primary - No Fusion Required)**
- **Effectiveness**: 8/10
- **Effort**: 1/10 (direct level-up reward)
- **Why**: 3.0 damage (highest primary), homing + boomerang
- **Strategy**: High single-target damage, reliable hits
- **Best for**: Boss fights and elites

#### **TIER A: Strong Early Picks**

**4. Fire (Primary)**
- **Effectiveness**: 7/10
- **Effort**: 1/10
- **Why**: 2.0 damage, burn DoT, group damage bonus (+0.5 per extra fire)
- **Strategy**: Stack multiple fires for synergy
- **Fusion potential**: Creates Steam, Lava, Smoke, Chaos

**5. Earth (Primary)**
- **Effectiveness**: 7/10
- **Effort**: 1/10
- **Why**: 3.0 damage (highest primary), knockback, shatters frozen enemies (2.5x)
- **Downside**: 3000ms cooldown (slow)
- **Strategy**: Pair with Ice for shatter combos

#### **TIER B: Situational**

**6. Water (Primary)**
- **Effectiveness**: 5/10
- **Effort**: 1/10
- **Why**: Only 1.5 damage, slow effect (0.5x speed)
- **Upside**: 1800ms cooldown (second fastest primary)
- **Strategy**: Use only if rushing to specific fusions (Storm, Crystal)

## Part 4: Late Game (Level 10+)

### Goal: Max Tier Elements + Passive Synergies

At this stage, focus shifts to:
1. **Tier 5 elements** (3.0x damage multiplier)
2. **Passive upgrades** (Phoenix Heart, Magnitude, etc.)
3. **Slot optimization** (drag best elements to primary slots)

### **Late Game Element Priorities (Tier to 5)**

#### **Priority 1: Tier Your Best Damage Dealer**
- **Meteor Tier 5** = 4.0 × 3.0 = **12.0 effective damage** (one-shots most enemies)
- **Crystal Tier 5** = 2.5 × 3.0 × 8 needles = **60.0 total damage spread**
- **Arcane Tier 5** = 3.0 × 3.0 = **9.0 effective damage** + homing

#### **Priority 2: Tier Your Fastest Element**
- **Air Tier 5** = 1.5 × 3.0 = 4.5 damage every 1200ms = **3.75 DPS**
- **Lightning Tier 5** = 2.0 × 3.0 = 6.0 damage every 1500ms = **4.0 DPS**

#### **Priority 3: Passive Upgrades**
Choose these at level-up instead of elements:
1. **Phoenix Heart** (revive) - Essential for survival
2. **Magnitude** (spell area increase) - More AOE coverage
3. **Power Amplification** (damage boost) - Scales with tiers
4. **Extra Slot** (6th element slot) - More variety
5. **Swift Stride** (movement speed) - Better dodging

## Part 7: Advanced Tips

### **Tier Stacking Strategy**
- Collecting the same element upgrades its tier automatically
- **Optimal**: Don't fuse until Tier 3+ on primaries
- **Example**: Lightning Tier 3 (4.0 effective) is better than many Tier 1 fusions

### **Slot Optimization**
- Drag your highest-DPS element to Slot 1 (gets best buffs)
- Put utility (slow, freeze) in Slots 4-6
- Arrange by priority: Damage → Speed → Utility

### **Passive Priority Order**
1. Phoenix Heart (first revive)
2. Extra Slot (6th element)
3. Magnitude (AOE increase)
4. Power Amplification
5. Passive Slot Increase
6. Swift Stride

### **When to Fuse vs When to Tier**
- **Fuse when**: You need specific utility (freeze, AOE, pierce)
- **Tier when**: Your current elements are already strong
- **Rule of thumb**: Tier primaries to 3, then consider fusions

## Conclusion

### **Universal Best Path (All Players)**
```
Levels 1-5: Build primary elements (Lightning, Air, Arcane)
Levels 6-8: Create 1-2 key fusions (Crystal and/or Meteor)
Levels 9-15: Tier everything to 3+
Levels 16-20: Tier main DPS to 5, add passives
Level 20+: Min-max with 6 slots, all Tier 4-5 elements
```

### **Key Principles**
1. ✅ **Primary elements are strong** - Don't rush fusions
2. ✅ **Crystal is best fusion** - Easiest and most effective
3. ✅ **Meteor is strongest single element** - But requires setup
4. ✅ **Tiers multiply power** - Tier 5 = 3x damage
5. ✅ **Air is fastest** - Best DPS for sustained damage
6. ✅ **Arcane is best boss killer** - Homing + high damage
